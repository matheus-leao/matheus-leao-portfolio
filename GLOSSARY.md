# Personal Portfolio

This context describes the professional website that presents Matheus Leão's career, writing, and service offerings to employers and prospective clients.

## Language

**Professional profile**:
A concise account of a person's professional identity, experience, education, and qualifications.
_Avoid_: Bio, résumé (for the whole website)

**Curriculum vitae**:
The structured record of professional experience, education, skills, and qualifications used to assess someone for a role.
_Avoid_: CV as a synonym for the entire portfolio

## Content

**Blog post**:
An article published on the website or a link to an article hosted elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Link (when referring to an original article)

**Service offering**:
A professional service Matheus makes available to clients, such as mentoring, training, or speaking.
_Avoid_: Product (unless it is a course sold as a product)

**Individual mentoring**:
One-to-one guidance for QA professionals seeking to strengthen their software quality practice.
_Avoid_: Coaching package (unless a defined package is offered)

**Team training**:
Instruction for an engineering or QA team on software quality and test automation practices.
_Avoid_: Course (unless a defined course is offered)

**Talk or workshop**:
A presentation or practical session about software quality, testing, or automation for an event or organization.
_Avoid_: Course (unless a defined course is offered)

**Contact inquiry**:
A message from a prospective employer, client, or event organizer asking to start a conversation.
_Avoid_: Booking (no appointment booking is planned for the initial site)

## Language support

**Site language**:
The language used to present the site's main pages; the initial languages are English and Portuguese, with English as the default.
_Avoid_: Locale (when referring to the reader-facing language)
